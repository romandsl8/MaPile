Hello, thank you for your review. Here is the requested information.

1. SCREEN RECORDING
A screen recording made on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS is attached in the "Attachment" field of this section. It starts with the app launch and shows the typical flow: searching and adding a book, scanning a barcode, the "Currently reading" tab with page progress, marking a book as finished with a rating, the Read / To-read galleries and the statistics.
MaPile has no account creation, no login, no paid content, and no user-generated content shared with other users in this version.

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
MaPile is a personal reading tracker for book lovers (general audience, French-speaking first). It solves a simple problem: keeping track of the books you want to read ("PAL" = to-read pile), the book you are currently reading (current page and progress) and the books you have finished, with your own rating and notes, plus reading statistics and a yearly goal. All data stays on the device.

3. HOW TO USE THE MAIN FEATURES (no login required)
- "Ajouter" tab (magnifying glass): type a title, an author or an ISBN (e.g. "Le Petit Prince"), open a result, tap "Ajouter à ma PAL". The "Scanner" button reads the ISBN barcode on the back of a printed book (camera).
- "PAL" tab: the to-read gallery. Open a book and tap "Commencer" to start reading it.
- "En cours" tab: enter the current page (or use +1/+10 buttons) to follow progress; tap "J'ai fini !" to mark it as read with a date and a rating.
- "Lus" tab: finished books, with your rating and review; book pages also show average reader ratings and public reader reviews from Apple Books.
- "Statistiques" tab: books and pages per day/week/month/quarter/year and the yearly goal.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Google Books API (book metadata, covers, ratings) – with an API key restricted to this app.
- Open Library API by Internet Archive (book metadata, covers, ratings).
- Apple's public iTunes Search API and public customer reviews RSS feed for Apple Books (metadata, HD covers, ratings, reader reviews).
- VisionKit DataScanner (on-device barcode scanning).
No analytics, no ads, no tracking, no authentication or payment service, no AI service.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions. Book information is requested in the device language/region (French editions are preferred on a French device). The app is not distributed in mainland China.

6. THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Book covers, summaries and ratings are retrieved at the user's request from the public APIs listed above and displayed with attribution ("Sources" in the About screen, and the source of each rating is shown). MaPile does not host or redistribute this content. No regulated industry is involved.

Thank you!
